\chapter{Conclusions}
\label{chap:conclusions}

\section{Summary}
\label{sec:conclusion-summary}

\begin{itemize}
    \item The GPU-sharing mechanism must be selected according to the workload's compute, memory, isolation, latency, and throughput requirements.
    \item DRA primarily provides allocation flexibility and operational benefits; it does not inherently improve application performance.
    \item Combining MIG and DRA is not universally optimal: MIG profiles and allocation policies must be tailored to specific workload requirements to maximize their benefits.
\end{itemize}

\section{Future Work}
\label{sec:future-work}

\begin{itemize}
    \item Investigate automatically enforced or automatically reconfigured MIG layouts according to cluster demand.
    \item Explore AI workload prediction and classification to select appropriate DRA-managed GPU or MIG resources before job admission.
\end{itemize}
